A deep-space probe in the tradition of Voyager, launched decades ago and still going. It is built from gold foil, aluminium struts and a dish antenna, and bolted to it is humanity's message to whoever finds it. That message is not a golden record. It is a copy of Goosnav Book 2 (the generic blue hardcover), a sealed Goosklerf pack, and a pneumatic canister into which new comics keep arriving by some unexplained means, one a month, forever.

It is shot like the most beautiful space documentary ever made: vast black space, a hard single sun, lens flares done honestly, slow majestic moves past gas giants and rings (build your own planets procedurally in the Goosklerf colours: a teal ice giant, an orange gas giant with storm bands, a purple ringed world), and the probe tiny against them, then enormous in macro.
