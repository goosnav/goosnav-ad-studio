YOUR CREATIVE LANE — "HOW IT IS MADE" (working title; improve it)

A colossal, lovingly lit, completely unmanned factory that manufactures Goosnav products. It is shot like a prestige industrial documentary, with slow dolly moves along conveyors, macro shots of machinery, and huge establishing shots of the hall. The narration is deadpan British documentary (Piper en_GB-alan or northern_english_male, processed to sound like a 1970s BBC film). The narrator starts calm and proud and slowly realises something is wrong: the factory has no workers, nobody remembers building it, and it appears to be making itself. He never panics. He just gets quieter.

Set-piece ideas (you decide; beat these if you can):
- Blank paper feeds off a reel the size of a building. An ink "rain" falls, and the real drawing sheets are stamped onto pages by a press, with sparks, steam and sodium light.
- Pages are gathered, bound and cased into Book 2 (generic blue hardcover, Helvetica title) by a giant press that slams in time with the soundtrack. Finished books ride the conveyor into infinity.
- The Goosklerf line: packs heat-crimped shut, dice tumbling out of a kiln glowing hot in the three Goosklerf colours, then quenched. Cards shuffle through a mechanical fan.
- The Patreon: a pneumatic tube fires this month's comics up and out through the roof, one a month, forever. The narrator calls it the "standing order".
- The reveal: a closer look shows one machine is building another machine. Keep the reveal ambiguous. Explaining it would make it worse.
- End on a quiet loading dock at dawn: Book 2, a Goosklerf pack and kit, and a Patreon comic on a pallet. Then prices, then the white card with the addresses.

Palette: raw concrete, sodium-vapour orange, deep shadows, haze and light shafts (Eevee volumetrics or cheap tricks), with Book Two blue (#0033A0) and the Goosklerf colours as the only saturated accents. Deliberately unlike the pastel paper desert of THE DILEMMA.

Format: 4:5 portrait (1080×1350). Compose every shot for portrait. Length: 60–90 s, your call. Title the deliverable with your final title.
