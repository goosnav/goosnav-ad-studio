# DRAW YOUR OWN (Book Two, 2026-10-22)

A quick follow-up to GET YOUR OWN on the same reading-desk set, using Book Two v2's actual
printed interior. The reader turns to printed comic 28, whose third panel is empty and
captioned "DRAW YOUR OWN PANEL". The friend asks to draw in it; the reader slams the book:
"Draw in your own." "I don't have one." Frame card: "Draw in your own copy" /
www.goosnav.shop. The product taste is a page you can only use in a copy you own.

15.9 s, 1080×1350, 24 fps, rendered fast (EEVEE, 8 samples, 540×675 native, 2x nearest).
Same pipeline as `films/get_your_own` (`make.sh`, `cut.json`, `sound.py`, `scene.py`,
`animate.py`, `render.py`, `finish.py`); `cut.json` holds the book leaf, lines and shots.
