# THE LONGEST TURN
### An 80-second film for Goosklerf, Goosnav Book 2 and the Goosnav Patreon
### Blender (Eevee), 4:5 portrait 1080×1350, 24 fps. Narrated.

(Working title was THE TABLE. *The Longest Turn* borrows the cadence of *The Longest Day*. The film
treats one turn of a card game as a D-Day landing.)

**Register.** A war newsreel crossed with Sunday-night sports. An American broadcaster (Piper
`en_US-joe-medium`, broadcast compression, a stadium slapback) calls a single game of Goosklerf
as if it were the most important battle in human history. He gives clock times like a war historian
("At nine fourteen, the fortresses went up") and never winks. Each rule he states is true: dice
decide combat, there are items, fortresses are shelter, and you win by taking the fortresses or
the enemy.

**World.** A tabletop the size of a continent, at night, in a thunderstorm. It is dark walnut and
soaking wet: the grain reads as terrain, the puddles hold the lightning, and rain streaks cross the
lens. Lightning strobes in the three Goosklerf colours: teal `#00B2A9`, orange `#FE5000` and purple
`#330072`. No pastel sky, no sodium light. The camera is a war-film camera: low, handheld, running
through trenches between cards, with crash zooms and one long slow-motion hero moment.

**Scale.** 1 Blender unit = 1 cm. Cards are 6.3 × 8.8, dice are 1.6, and the table is 200 × 110.
During the battle the camera sits 0.3–3 cm above the wood with wide lenses and no depth of field,
so everything reads as enormous. In the reveal the camera rises to human height and depth of field
turns on, so the same table reads as small.

**Products (owner rule: generic renders).** Cards use `gen_card.png` (alpha clip) and the pack uses
`gen_pack.png`. Dice use the dice textures. Book 2 is the generic blue `#0033A0` hardcover
(`gen_book_*`). The rulebook is a new generic cover: deep purple with the crisp `GOOSKLERF.jpg`
logo and Helvetica "Goosklerf Rulebook". The only Goosnav drawings are the real comic pages
(`comic_XX.png`) on the Patreon dispatch.

### 1. THE NIGHT BEFORE — 0:00–0:07
Black. Distant thunder and rain. A teal lightning strobe reveals a wet wooden plain running to the
horizon. Far away, card fortresses stand against the clouds. A second strobe, orange, lights the
puddles. The camera drifts forward at ankle height, handheld. Title, small, in Helvetica:
**THE LONGEST TURN**.
> NARRATOR: It was a weeknight. Two players... one table.

### 2. THE FORTRESSES GO UP — 0:07–0:15.5
The camera runs forward low across the wood. Cards lying flat on the table slam upright one after
another, left and right, each a wall hundreds of feet tall. Each slam throws spray and shakes the
lens. The camera ducks into the trench between two walls. Chyron: **21:14**.
> NARRATOR: At nine fourteen, the fortresses went up. In this game, a fortress is shelter... and
> shelter is everything.

### 3. REINFORCEMENTS — 0:15.5–0:24.5
Wide and low. A Goosklerf booster pack lies on the plain like a dam. Its seam strains, then the top
tears off and spins away, and the cards burst out like floodwater: twelve giant cards sliding and
tumbling across the wet table towards the lens, throwing spray. The last one passes overhead and
blacks out the frame.
> NARRATOR: Then... reinforcements. Twelve cards to a pack. And every one of them came to fight.

### 4. THE DICE COME DOWN — 0:24.5–0:34.5
The camera tilts up into the storm clouds. Three dice (teal, orange, purple) fall out of the
clouds like meteors, trailing fire. Crash zoom. They hit one after another, cratering the wood with
a flash, a shockwave ring, splinters and steam. The camera cowers in the trench under the card
walls. Chyron: **21:20**.
> NARRATOR: At nine twenty, the dice came down. In Goosklerf, dice decide combat. Nobody ever said
> the dice had to be small.

### 5. THE RULEBOOK — 0:34.5–0:42.5
The rulebook stands upright on the plain like a cathedral front: purple, the logo, "Goosklerf
Rulebook". Its cover swings open like a great door, and warm gold light pours out across the wet
wood and through the rain.
> NARRATOR: Somebody opened the rulebook. It said what it has always said. Roll for combat. Use
> your items. Take the fortresses... or take the enemy.

### 6. THE FIVE — 0:42.5–0:54.5
The hero moment. One orange die is thrown and tumbles in extreme slow motion, close enough to fill
the frame, through suspended rain. Lightning strobes teal, orange and purple on its faces. The sound
drops to a heartbeat and a held tone. It hits the wet wood, bounces once and settles: **five**. A
stadium roar. Behind it, a card fortress topples wall by wall.
> NARRATOR: And then... the roll.
> NARRATOR (after the landing): It was a five. Nobody alive had seen a five like it. The fortress
> fell at nine thirty-one.

### 7. THE STORM PASSES — 0:54.5–1:04
The rain thins and the lightning stops. The camera pulls up and up from the die, and depth of field
arrives: the continent is a dining table in an ordinary room, lit by one warm pendant lamp. There
are two chairs, rain on a dark window, and a door with a line of hallway light under it. Scattered
cards and dice lie where they fell. On the corner of the table sits Goosnav Book 2, the blue
hardcover. Chyron: **Goosnav Book 2 — hardcover**.
> NARRATOR: Then the storm passed... the way storms do. What was left was a table, in a room, on a
> weeknight.
> NARRATOR: And beside it, the history of all of it. Hardcover.

### 8. THE DISPATCH — 1:04–1:09.5
Floor level at the door. A Goosnav comic (a real comic page) slides in under the door through the
line of hallway light and stops at the lens. Chyron: **The Patreon — dispatches, monthly**.
> NARRATOR: And under the door... dispatches. Monthly.

### 9. THE TABLE — 1:09.5–1:20
The hero shot, in portrait: on the lamplit table, Goosnav Book 2 lies flat, with the booster pack,
the rulebook and three dice. The product lines come up in Helvetica Bold, stated like measurements:
- Goosklerf. Packs $13.95. Kits from $29.95.
- Goosnav Book 2. $19.95.
- The Patreon. $3 a month.
- www.goosnav.shop

> NARRATOR: Goosklerf. Book Two. The Patreon. W W W dot goosnav dot shop.

At 1:16.8, the white card: **Your roll.** above www.goosnav.shop and www.patreon.com/goosnav (Times,
`#0000EE`, underlined, bottom left).
> NARRATOR: Your roll.

## Sound
- **Storm bed:** rain (band-passed noise, stereo, gusting), distant rolling thunder, and close
  thunder cracks on every lightning strobe. Each strobe is written to `events.json`.
- **Score:** low war-film strings (detuned saw stacks, D minor), timpani (low slams) under the
  fortress and dice sections, a brass-like swell for the rulebook light.
- **Hits:** card slams (slam and splash), the pack tear (ripping noise, whoosh, a flood of
  flutter), meteor dice (descending whistle, boom, splinter crackle, debris).
- **Slow motion:** everything drops out except a heartbeat, a sub drone and a held high tone. The
  landing is a huge die hit, then a synthesised stadium roar.
- **Room:** the storm cuts to near-silence: rain on a window, a clock ticking, lamp hum. The comic
  slide is a soft paper swish.
- **Voice:** Piper `en_US-joe-medium`, tape 0.97, broadcast chain (high-pass 140 Hz, presence lift,
  fast compression) and a 105 ms stadium slapback. Voice ≥ 8 dB over effects. Master −14 LUFS.
