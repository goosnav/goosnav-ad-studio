# UNDER THE DOOR

*Working title was "3:14 A.M." Final title: **UNDER THE DOOR**, because the tide comes in under it, and then you are under it too.*

4:5 portrait, 1080×1350, 24 fps, about 56 s. Blender Eevee. The Patreon leads; the book and the game follow.

**Palette:** deep night blue (Book Two blue `#0033A0` dominates the water), warm amber lamp light, paper white. Nothing pastel, nothing sodium, no storm.

**Narrator:** a calm female voice (Piper `en_GB-jenny_dioco-medium`), close and intimate, a small soft room. A bedtime story that is slightly wrong. Short sentences. Long silences.

**Music:** all synthesized. A slow music box (plucked, detuned, a little out of time) over an underwater pad. Paper hush for water. A low heartbeat-slow swell under the flood. Dawn: the pad opens into a major chord and the music box plays its last four notes.

## Real vs built

- Real: the seven 2026 comic boards (`comic_00`–`comic_06`, scans cropped and levelled by `studio.textures`). The hero comic left by the door is `comic_03` ("Sleeping wastes so much time"). Underwater, the scans get a render-time tint so the ink glows faintly; nothing is redrawn.
- Built / generic: the room, lamp, clock, bed, door, water, paper tower, Book 2 (generic blue with Helvetica), the Goosklerf card (`gen_card`) and pack (`gen_pack`), dice.
