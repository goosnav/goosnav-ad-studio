# THE DILEMMA
### A 60-second film for Goosnav Book Two, Goosklerf, and the Goosnav Patreon
### Blender (Eevee), 1920×1080, 24 fps. Narrated.

Register: a cosmic desert prophecy, told by a narrator who is completely sure of himself and
entirely wrong about everything except the prices. *Xavier: Renegade Angel* by way of a 1970s
planetarium show. Big, saturated, a little uncanny. The only real artwork on screen is Goosnav's:
the Book Two cover, pages of real drawings, real Goosklerf cards, the real pack. Everything else is
landscape, weather, and light.

World: an endless desert whose dunes are stacked paper. A candy sky, violet at the top, hot pink
at the horizon. Three suns hang low, one in each Goosklerf colour: teal, orange, purple.

Narrator: low, slow, reverent, too close to the microphone, drenched in a cathedral reverb.

> Revision (2026-09-26, owner note): the book is now a generic Book Two blue (#0033A0) hardcover with white Helvetica "Goosnav / Book 2", not the photographed Dilemma cover. The Goosklerf cards and pack use the crisp flat logo file instead of product photos.

---

### 1. THE MONOLITH — 0:00–0:09

Black. One drip. Wind swells up out of nothing.

Hard cut to the paper desert. Dunes to the horizon, layered like the edges of a ream. The three suns
sit on the skyline. On the nearest dune stands a single book, fifteen feet tall, upright like a
monolith: **Goosnav Book Two**, pale blue boards, the Dilemma cover facing us. Paper grit blows off
the dune crests. The camera drifts toward it very slowly, at knee height.

> NARRATOR: Before the book... there was the dilemma.

> NARRATOR: A man had made more drawings than a man can keep. So he pressed them flat, between two
> blue walls, and called it a book.

### 2. THE DOOR — 0:09–0:16

Close on the cover. Light begins leaking from the page edges, a white seam around the fore-edge.
The book shudders. Paper grains hop on the dune around it. The suns pulse.

> NARRATOR: But a book is only a door that has decided to stay closed. And a door... can change its mind.

### 3. THE DETONATION — 0:16–0:21

The book **explodes**. The front board spins off toward camera. Hundreds of pages burst outward in a
sphere, slow-motion, each one printed with a real Goosnav drawing — Foot Oiling Machine, Emotion
Nullifier, Automated Gnome Armorer, Shoe Multipliers, Self-Filling Coffee Mug. A white shockwave
ring rolls across the dunes. Everything blooms. Sound drops to a single deep boom and a ringing tone.

### 4. THE WEATHER — 0:21–0:31

The pages don't fall. They are caught in a vortex and pulled into a column of paper that climbs into
the pink sky. The camera is pulled in with them and corkscrews upward through the funnel. Drawings
whip past the lens close enough to read.

> NARRATOR: One year of drawings. A machine for oiling feet. A machine for nullifying emotion.
> A mug that fills itself, and asks nothing in return.

> NARRATOR: They had been flat for so long. Now they were weather.

### 5. THE CLOSED SET — 0:31–0:42

At the top of the column the pages fold. They come down as **Goosklerf cards**, spinning, tumbling,
card backs and card faces flashing. Three enormous dice — teal, orange, purple — fall past the camera
end over end. The cards hit the dunes and stand up on their edges, slamming into place one after
another until they form a fortress. The dice land beside it, bounce, roll, stop.

> NARRATOR: Some of the drawings became soldiers. Some became fortresses. Some became dice, which is
> what a drawing becomes when it no longer wishes to have an opinion.

> NARRATOR: This is Goosklerf. A card game. Two players. You take the fortress, or you take each other.

### 6. THE STANDING ORDER — 0:42–0:51

Tilt up. A slot has opened in the sky, a long glowing rectangle like a mail slot in God's front door.
New pages are falling out of it, slowly, endlessly, like snow — this year's comics, still arriving.
One drifts down into the lens and fills the frame.

> NARRATOR: And the drawings did not stop. They have still not stopped. Every month, more of them fall
> out of the sky. For three dollars a month, you may stand underneath.

### 7. THE RETURN — 0:51–1:00

Everything reverses. The pages rush back down into a single point on the dune. The book reassembles,
slams shut with a boom that shakes the camera, and comes to rest on the sand, with the Goosklerf
pack leaning against it and the card fortress behind. The three suns line up behind the book.
Slow orbit. Hero light.

> NARRATOR: The dilemma remains. It is hardcover now.

End card, over the hero shot, plain and still:

**GOOSNAV BOOK TWO. GOOSKLERF. THE PATREON.**
`www.goosnav.shop`
`www.patreon.com/goosnav`

> NARRATOR: w w w dot goosnav dot shop.

One last page drifts down and lands flat against the lens. Black.

> NARRATOR (a whisper): Goosnav continues.

## Notes on the rules

- Real artwork only. Every drawing, card, cover and pack in frame is a photograph or scan of the real
  object. Nothing is drawn, redrawn, generated, or extended. The 3D work is weather, sand, and light.
- Claims: Book Two is hardcover, signed, $19.95, 2025 drawings (the shop says so). Goosklerf: dice,
  fortresses, two players (the shop and rulebook say so). Patreon: $3/month, new art monthly (the shop
  says so). No comic count is stated because it is not verified.
- URLs always with `www.`.
