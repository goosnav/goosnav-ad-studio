# Reusable product-taste film brief

Use this with a coding/creative agent that has access to the studio and the relevant product assets. Fill in the actual product and sources; leave unknown claims unknown. It is a brief, not a rigid screenplay template or an executable harness config.

---

Make an entertaining Goosnav film advertising **[exact product/edition]**.

Read `AGENTS.md`, `PLAYBOOK.md`, and `docs/product-content-first.md` first. Use the repo's tools and existing assets where they help; do not let the compact generator's default runtime or speed preset determine the idea.

**Source material:** [actual product pages/card/rule files or asset IDs and locators]. Verify that selected material belongs to this product. If the necessary source is missing, identify the gap rather than inventing what is inside it.

**Creative objective:** Give viewers a taste of this product. Derive the premise, observations, reactions and curiosity from its actual contents or experience. Do not make a generic pitch, an unrelated sketch with a pasted-on title, or an abstract declaration that the book is mysterious/funny/important. Make something worth watching, with an honest path from that experience to wanting the product.

Use GET YOUR OWN's source/log as an example of product-grounded curiosity, not a formula to copy. Do not assume its production log means you watched its final video.

**Timing:** Write the scene and test its performance before choosing the final runtime. No arbitrary seconds target or production deadline applies unless I explicitly provide one. Remove unearned time; retain meaningful reactions and intelligible speech. If the compact harness cannot represent the chosen story cleanly, use the authored film/toolkit path. Do not misreport durations or bypass safety/budget controls.

**Production:** Preserve authentic Goosnav artwork. Verify character/prop capabilities and source membership. Reuse good voices, assets and accepted work. Preview uncertain staging before expensive rendering. Make sound, cuts and acting serve the scene. Do not rebuild infrastructure or rerender successful work without a concrete reason.

**Before finishing:** Review product fidelity, dialogue, reaction timing, visible sample, buyer understanding, actual destination, and whether the piece is entertaining independently of the CTA. Report any unverified audiovisual judgment honestly. Technical export success alone is not creative approval.

Deliver the finished media and editable source according to the configured delivery method, a compact source/claims packet, and brief review notes. Record actual production time/cost as learning data; do not claim audience or sales improvement without measured results. Do not publish publicly or spend beyond my existing authorization.
