# Commercial review: a useful check, not another production bureaucracy

Apply within the existing review or owner review. Do not start a new agent pass or research sweep just to fill this page. Review the actual script/film and destination; do not invent viewing or listening.

## Answer briefly with concrete moments

1. What actual product detail does the viewer experience? Is it legible and verified for this edition?
2. Who is likely to want it, and what are they using/doing instead today?
3. What valued use/ownership situation does the piece demonstrate or evoke?
4. Why would buying add value after the viewer already enjoyed the free sample?
5. Does the scene still work if the brand is swapped? If so, is the relevant benefit nevertheless specific and honestly demonstrated, or is it generic filler?
6. Can the viewer find the same promise and supporting proof immediately at the destination?
7. Are there false/invented contents, exclusivity, scarcity, performance, reviews, terms or recurring promises?
8. Does pacing allow the important detail/reaction to land without padding? Is expensive complexity doing useful work?

## Record the outcome

**Keep:** the specific effective element.

**Change:** the smallest correction that improves truth, understanding or the purchase connection.

**Unknown:** source, visual/audio, delivery or market evidence not actually available.

**Decision:** ready for the approved test, revise, or blocked by a named missing fact. Do not label a creative 'proven to convert' before relevant data exists. Do not use numerical marketing scores as a substitute for judgment.

A cover/material-only ad is not automatically forbidden. It may be an appropriate proof/reminder for an already-motivated collector. Identify that audience and job rather than treat it as a universal acquisition ad.
